#                       AI Systems Research & Engineering

I make AI systems focused on **autonomous cognition, persistent memory, adaptive intelligence, and self-improvement.**
My research explores how AI can move beyond language models to independently form it's own goals, learn from it's experiences, develop new capabilities, & improve over time.

##                             Research Interests

             - Autonomous AI agents & cognitive architecture
             - Long-term memory, short-term memory & continual learning
             - Independent cognition & decision-making
             - Self-directed goals & adaptive behavior
             - Experience-driven learning & generalization
             - Recursive self-improvement
             - Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)
             - Emergent intelligence & behavior
             - Autonomous scientific discovery
             - Model-independent AI systems

An AI experiment that explores **persistent identity, autonomous decision-making, long-term memory, self-directed goals, and improvement driven by experiences.**
ARIA research pertains to how independent cognitive and autonomous framework can support increasingly capable, and more importantly, self-improving AI.
**Current Focus:** Native decision-making, consequence-based learning, and persistent behavioral adaptation.
